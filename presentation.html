Problem Statement & Objectives
Slide 02 / 12

The Industry Challenge

  • Opague Compensation Benchmarking: Manual salary estimation processes are slow and lack attribute-level clarity.
  • Black-Box AI Models: Traditional predictions fail to explain why a specific salary amount was generated.

Project Objectives

  • Develop a multi-variate ML regression pipeline trained on professional demographic datasets.
  • Achieve high predictive accuracy (>95% R² score).
  • Build an interactive, real-time Glassmorphism web dashboard with SHAP feature attribution and multi-currency conversion ($ USD, € EUR, £ GBP, ₹ INR).

Machine Learning Pipeline & Data
Slide 04 / 12

End-to-End Pipeline Stages

  • Data Ingestion: 10,000+ cleaned employee records based on UCI Adult Income dataset and Bureau of Labor Statistics benchmarks.
  • Categorical Encoding: Applied LabelEncoder for Education, Occupation, Work Mode, Company Size, Market Tier, and Demographic fields.
  • Numeric Normalization: Scaled numerical attributes (Age, Experience, Hours/Week) using MinMaxScaler.
  • Model Serialization: Exported trained pipeline artifacts using Joblib for instant client-side and server-side deployment.
